Our story

The house of Itán

Itán means “story”. This is ours — told the only way we know: through fire.

01 — The concept

Nigeria’s larder,a fine-dining lens.

Itán opened in 2019 with a simple conviction: the flavours Nigerians already love — smoke, pepper, ferment, char — belong on the world’s finest tables. We take the dishes of home kitchens, roadside grills and Sunday afternoons, and rebuild them plate by plate, without losing their soul.

02 — Food philosophy

Fire & patience.

  • 01

    Open fire first

    Everything touches flame, smoke or ember before it touches a plate.

  • 02

    Grown close

    Produce from farms within 200 km; fish day-boats only; spices stone-ground in-house.

  • 03

    Nothing wasted

    Trim becomes stock, stock becomes jus, ash becomes the salt on your bread.

  • 04

    Time as seasoning

    Our ferments, smokes and age-outs run for days — flavour you can’t rush.

“We don’t serve food.We serve memories, plated.”

— Ifeoma Alabi, Founder

03 — The room

Candlelight, oak& low conversation.

Twenty-four seats around one long oak table, a bar of blackened steel, and light kept deliberately low. The room is quiet enough for confession and warm enough for celebration — dress as you are; the food does the performing.

04 — The hands

A kitchen of twelve,trained at the pass.

Every cook at Itán spends their first month on the grill, learning our fire. Chefs stage in Copenhagen and Lagos, return with technique, and leave their egos at the door. What reaches you is tasted by at least three pairs of hands first.

    Quick answers

    Do you take walk-ins?

    Yes — the eight ember-bar seats are kept for walk-ins every night. Arrive before 19:00 for the shortest wait.

    Can you cater to allergies?

    Almost always. Tell us when booking and the kitchen will adjust the menu; nut and shellfish-free tasting routes are standard.

    Is there a dress code?

    Smart-casual, come as you are. The room is air-conditioned and occasionally smoky from the pass — it’s part of the theatre.
